About Me

I am a Research Engineer at Taobao & Tmall Group, Alibaba Inc., focused on building multimodal methods for content understanding and generative recommendation.

I received my Ph.D. from the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) in January 2025, supervised by Prof. Aasa Feragen, and affiliated with the Pioneer Centre of Artificial Intelligence. My research centers on multimodal learning — bridging time series, images, and point clouds — through robust neural architectures, explainability, and clinically-oriented applications. During my Ph.D. I was a visiting researcher in Prof. Qi Dou's group at the Chinese University of Hong Kong.

I earned my B.E. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (2019), where I worked with Prof. Jun Wu. My primary interests include multimodal learning, medical imaging, and interpretable models for real-world systems.
